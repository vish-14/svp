Frequently Asked Questions

Is this program suitable for fresh graduates?

Absolutely. In fact, most of our students are recent graduates looking to gain practical studio experience that universities often miss.

What kind of placement assistance is provided?

We provide end-to-end support including portfolio review, mock interviews, and direct referrals to our 50+ hiring partners.

Will I receive a certification upon completion?

Yes, you will receive a Professional Architecture Mastery certification which is recognized by our network of partner firms.

Is the training available online?

We offer a hybrid model with both live virtual sessions and in-person studio workshops for hands-on learning.
